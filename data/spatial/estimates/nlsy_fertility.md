## Definition and sample

- Outcome: ever had a biological child = max over rounds of NUMKIDyy ("number of children ever born", 1980/1982-2022) and the cross-round NUMKID is >= 1. NUMKID80 is the 1980 info-sheet item; it is included in the max (diagnostic: 1346 respondents report >= 1 child in 1980, 16 of them report 0 in every later round).
- Sample: `nlsy_ability.POP_SAMPLES` respondents (cross-sectional + retained supplemental samples; military and dropped supplements excluded), born 1957-1964 (Q1-3_A~Y, two-digit years); completed-fertility sample = last valid (NUMKID, AGEATINT) observation at age >= 45; both genders pooled; weight = SAMPWEIGHT/100 of that last round (zero/missing dropped).
- Mapping to the model: p = share of adults (both genders) who ever have a child; model child arrives at the transition to adulthood with probability p.

## Male under-reporting of fathered children

Women 83.1% vs men 77.5% ever had a child (gap +5.6 pp). Surveys of men are known to miss fathered children (births outside marriage, non-resident fathers, children of earlier relationships); the NSFG shows the same pattern (men 76.5% vs women 84.3% at 40-49). The model's p applies to both genders, so the women's rate is an upper bound for adults and the men's rate a lower bound; the pooled rate is the default.
